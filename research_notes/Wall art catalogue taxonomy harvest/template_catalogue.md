# The template catalogue — extracted from all 1,463,031 listings

Mined from the full corpus, not a sample. For each template the **slot values
were extracted rather than guessed**, so these lists are the generator's input.

The headline: their catalogues run on roughly twenty phrasings. Several are
excellent, fully pictorial, need no lettering — and are filled to a tiny
fraction of their capacity.

## The four finds worth building on

### 1. `Painting Of A {PET} In {FAMOUS GARDEN}` — the best template they have

Extracted slot values: *dog in royal botanic gardens* (45), *cat in royal
botanic gardens* (34), *dog in kew gardens* (30), *cat in kew gardens* (22),
*cat in eden project* (24), *dog in eden project* (22), *dog in isola bella*
(24), *cat in isola bella* (22), *dog in tivoli gardens* (19), *cat in tivoli
gardens* (18), *dog in versailles gardens* (18), *dog in shanghai botanical
garden* (19), *dog in gothenburg botanical garden* (18), *dog in brooklyn
botanic garden* (18).

It is a clean **{pet} × {garden}** cross. Fully pictorial, no lettering, no
geometry. And they have run it over perhaps a dozen gardens worldwide.

Britain is the gardening country and the list is enormous: Kew, Wisley,
Sissinghurst, Hidcote, Stourhead, Bodnant, Alnwick Garden, Lost Gardens of
Heligan, Great Dixter, Trelissick, Bodnant, Levens Hall, Chatsworth, Blenheim,
Hever, Nymans, Wakehurst, Harlow Carr, Rosemoor, Bridgewater — plus every
National Trust garden. Cross that against **221 Kennel Club dog breeds and 45
cat breeds** and the cell count runs to tens of thousands from one template.

### 2. `Painting Of {CITY} With A Cat` — ~100 listings each, world cities only

*havana* (100), *berlin* (100), *rome* (98), *vienna* (98), *barcelona* (98),
*prague* (98), *marrakech* (98), *florence* (96), *venice* (94), *buenos
aires* (94), *sydney* (94), *new york* (92), *amsterdam* (92), *paris* (92),
*london* (90).

Note the depth — about 100 variants **per city** — against the breadth: roughly
twenty cities, and **London is the only British one**. The same template across
1,408 UK settlements is open ground, and the cat is doing real work here: it
gives a cityscape a character and a reason to look.

The same phrase also runs over flowers: *ranunculus* (99), *hydrangea* (96),
*sunflower* (95), *marigold* (93), *dahlia* (91), *amaryllis* (91),
*daffodils* (89). So it is really `{subject} with a cat`, and the cat is a
universal multiplier they apply to anything.

### 3. `Travel Poster For {CITY}` — only 103 cities in the entire corpus

*oslo* (21), *bangkok* (20), *helsinki* (20), *reykjavik* (20), *marrakech*
(18), *bangalore* (18), *belgrade* (18), *kyiv* (18), **liverpool (18)**,
*warsaw* (18), *bratislava* (17), *sofia* (17), *positano* (17), *riga* (17),
*venice* (16), *sarajevo* (16), *istanbul* (16).

**Liverpool is the only UK entry.** One hundred and three cities, worldwide,
and they stopped. Every UK city, town, National Park and coastal resort is
unclaimed in this template.

### 4. `{X} Skyline` — 2,018 cities, and Canada is covered better than Britain

*new york city* (280), *chicago* (228), *london* (116), *cape town* (85),
*dubai* (83), **toronto canada (76)**, *singapore* (75), **montreal canada
(73)**, *san francisco* (65), *melbourne* (62), **vancouver canada (56)**,
**edmonton canada (55)**, **winnipeg canada (53)**.

Edmonton and Winnipeg have more skyline listings than almost any British city
outside London. Somebody ran the Canadian list properly and nobody has run the
British one.

## Supporting lists, extracted

**`In The Style Of {X}` — 211 values.** pop art (870), matisse (746), ukiyo-e
(304), impressionism (254), cubism (181), expressionism (168), surrealism
(154), gustav klimt (150), watercolour (114), william morris (112), fauvism
(108), abstract expressionism (73), post modernism (72), renaissance (71), art
nouveau (67), monet (66), minimalism (56), van gogh (55).

**`Inspired By {X}` — 292 values.** paul cezanne (156), matisse (114+26),
van gogh (22), warhol (20), the birth of venus (16), edward hopper (13), claude
monet (12), william morris (12), klimt (12), camille pissarro (12), the death
of marat (12). **Warhol and Hopper are still in copyright and should not be
named.**

**Environment phrases — a single family.** `{subject} in the forest` (fox 218,
deer 148, waterfall 107, wolf 50, owl 37, mushrooms 37), `on the beach` (sunset
368, palm trees 71, boat 44, seashells 27, sea turtle 26), `in the mountains`
(sunset 538, lake 92, sunrise 71, autumn 67, village 58, cabin 22), `in the
garden` (cat 137, flowers 124, orchid 120, roses 88, tulips 51, daisies 45).

That is really one template — **`{subject} in {environment}`** — and the
environment list is short and reusable. Britain supplies better environments
than "the forest": the fells, the moors, a bluebell wood, a harbour, a
drystone wall, a hedgerow, a chalk cliff, a sea loch.

**`{X} In A Vase` — flowers (1,770)**, tulips 145, daisies 87, sunflowers 71,
roses 69, peonies 47, poppies 44, daffodils 22. A still-life template with
almost no species depth; the RHS list and 1,692 British native plants dwarf it.

**`Ohara Koson Inspired Bird Painting` — 1,375 listings.** Koson died in 1945,
so he is safely out of copyright, and this is a single-artist template run to
depth. The same could be done with any pre-1956 printmaker.

## Two warnings from the data

**Their titles are contaminated.** `aihrgdesign` — a vendor name — appears as a
slot value **999 times** in the travel-poster template and **629 times** in the
mid-century one, and opens 6,874 titles outright. `nissan` appears 169 times in
the skyline template. Copying their slot lists blindly would import this.

**Their titles are truncated mid-word, at scale.** The last-word frequency
table shows `p` (16,754), `prin` (15,065), `pri` (14,360), `pr` (13,843), `ar`
(11,485) — roughly **90,000 titles** cut off mid-word by a builder that
trimmed to 80 characters without respecting word boundaries. Ours must not.
