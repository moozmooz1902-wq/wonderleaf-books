# Image-based wall art: which subjects, styles and palettes actually sell (2025-2026, UK end market)

**Scope note:** typography and quote prints are out of scope. Everything below is image-led.

**Source-quality warning, read this first.** There is almost no openly published *primary* bestseller data for wall art. Marketplaces (Etsy, Desenio, Juniqe, Posterlounge, Society6, Displate) publish bestseller *pages* but not sales numbers, and their category pages are heavily JavaScript-rendered and partly unfetchable (Desenio's UK bestseller list returned HTTP 429 on fetch). A large share of what ranks for "best selling wall art" queries is SEO content marketing by print-on-demand vendors and AI-written aggregator sites (accio.com, insightagent.app, printkk.com, shieldmyshop.com). Where a number comes from one of those, I have labelled it as unverified. Treat the search-volume figures in particular as directional, not as audited data. The individual-listing evidence (review counts, "X purchases in the past month", shop lifetime sales) is weaker per data point but is at least observed marketplace behaviour rather than vendor marketing.

## Q1. Which image categories sell as wall art, ranked by evidence of demand?

### Takeaway
The repeatedly-cited demand ranking puts nursery/kids first by search volume, then abstract, then botanical/nature, then boho earth-tone, vintage/retro, and celestial. Botanical, landscape, abstract-in-earth-tones and "oversized/statement" are the four that appear in *every* independent 2026 forecast, which makes them the safest core of a catalogue; celestial, maps and personalised/place-based work appear as solid but narrower.

### Cited Findings
- Claimed monthly Etsy search volumes, ranked: nursery and kids' wall art 140,000+; abstract art prints 90,000+; botanical and nature prints 40,000+; boho and earthy-tone prints 35,000+; vintage and retro posters 30,000+; celestial and astrology art 20,000+ — [Accio, Best Selling Wall Art Etsy](https://www.accio.com/business/best-selling-wall-art-on-etsy). **Unverified aggregator; methodology not stated. The same page also lists typography/quote and faith categories, which are out of scope here.**
- Google-style keyword volumes cited for the category: "wall art for living room" 22,200/month, "abstract wall art" 12,100/month, "wall art prints" 5,400/month — [Printseekers / POD wall art companies](https://www.printseekers.com/blog-post/top-8-print-on-demand-wall-art-companies). Unverified.
- eRank's 2026 Etsy forecast names, as the wall-art-relevant keywords to build on: "botanical prints", "landscapes", "abstract art in earth tones", "nature-inspired art", "oversized wall art", "moss wall art", "textured wall pieces", "textile art", "vintage artwork" — [eRank, Etsy Trends 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/etsy-trends-2026/)
- Printify's 2026 Etsy forecast names the same core plus specifics: "botanical prints", "vintage botanical prints", "wave line art", "abstract wall art", "framed cover art" (book-cover art, driven by BookTok) — [Printify, Leading trends for Etsy 2026](https://printify.com/blog/leading-trends-for-etsy-sellers-in-2026/)
- "Moss wall art" showed a +15,046% month-on-month search-volume change in August, the largest home-decor mover reported — [Printify](https://printify.com/blog/leading-trends-for-etsy-sellers-in-2026/)
- Maps: "custom-style maps of cities, neighbourhoods or landmarks" are called out as extremely popular as personalised gifts and housewarming presents — [MerchOne, Profitable POD niches](https://merchone.com/blog/profitable-print-on-demand-niches/)
- Pet portraits and pet memorial art are given an average order value of $50-95, the same band as wedding/anniversary personalisation — [MerchOne](https://merchone.com/blog/profitable-print-on-demand-niches/)
- Botanical and nature prints are attributed to the houseplant boom rather than to an art trend — [MerchOne](https://merchone.com/blog/profitable-print-on-demand-niches/)
- UK trade (gallery supplying interior designers) frames 2026 demand as statement canvases at bold scale, textured and mixed-media pieces, and layered gallery walls scaled to sightline and furniture width — [Trowbridge, UK wall art trends 2026](https://www.trowbridgegallery.com/trade-art-insights/current-uk-wall-art-trends-for-2026-influencing-designers-4733)
- Market context: wall art and canvas print market put at $74.2bn in 2025 rising to $117.2bn by 2034 (5.22% CAGR), with print-on-demand art the fastest-growing segment at 23-25% CAGR — [Rossetti Art, canvas print statistics](https://rossettiart.com/blogs/news/canvas-print-wall-art-statistics); similar figures at [Magnetic Art](https://www.magneticart.art/blog/wall-art-home-decor-statistics). Both are retailer blogs citing unnamed sources.

### Inferences
- A defensible evidence-weighted ranking for a UK image-only catalogue: **(1) botanical/florals, (2) abstract shapes and earth-tone abstraction, (3) nursery/children's illustration, (4) landscapes incl. countryside, (5) coastal/seascape, (6) animals and birds (UK-native weighted), (7) vintage ephemera / antique plates, (8) celestial, (9) maps and city plans, (10) still life, (11) architecture, (12) line art/single-line figures, (13) food and drink, (14) music, (15) cars/motorcycles, (16) aviation, (17) sport.** The bottom five are not absent from the market but attract no mention in any 2026 forecast found, and they skew male/hobbyist, which is the opposite of the buyer profile every source describes.
- The categories in the brief that I found **no positive 2026 demand evidence for at all** are sport, aviation, cars/motorcycles and music. That is a real signal of low priority, not merely a gap: multiple forecasts enumerate ten-plus wall-art subjects and none includes them.
- "Oversized/statement" is a *format* signal appearing in both the eRank keyword set and the UK trade view. It argues for generating at aspect ratios and resolutions that survive A1/A0 and large canvas, not just A4/A3.

### Gaps
- No audited unit-sales or revenue ranking by subject from any marketplace. Every ranking found is either keyword volume or vendor assertion.
- Society6, Juniqe and Posterlounge bestseller data could not be obtained; Desenio's UK bestseller page refused fetch (429).
- No Pinterest Trends or Google Trends export was retrievable in this pass, so the search-volume claims could not be independently cross-checked.

## Q3. Which visual styles and treatments sell?

### Takeaway
2026 style demand converges on texture, visible hand-making and warmth, and away from flat digital minimalism. The named style movements to generate against are vintage/antique restoration, textured and impasto, watercolour, abstract earth-tone brushwork, photographic fine art, and reduced minimal line work; plus a cluster of 2026 aesthetic labels (Chateaucore, MIDImalism, maritime living, island luxe, old-money romance).

### Cited Findings
- 2026 mood on Etsy is "intentional": buyers gravitate to products that feel human, with texture, story and soul; decor moves to calm spaces that feel warm, lived-in and personal — [Outfy, top selling Etsy home decor](https://www.outfy.com/blog/top-selling-etsy-home-decor-trends/) and [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/etsy-trends-2026/)
- Named style treatments in eRank's 2026 forecast: "freehand brushstrokes", "ceramic-like finishes", "textured wall pieces", "textile art", "lived-in finishes" — [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/etsy-trends-2026/)
- Named 2026 aesthetic movements: **Chateaucore** (old-world glamour, ornate romantic detail), **old-money romance** (timeless, minimal, premium), **MIDImalism** (neutral base plus one standout element), **literary girl** (BookTok-driven), **island luxe** (sun-washed simplicity, natural materials), **galactic metallic** (high-shine nostalgia/futurism), **maritime living** (rope detail, weathered blues) — [Printify](https://printify.com/blog/leading-trends-for-etsy-sellers-in-2026/); Chateaucore and MIDImalism independently named as 2025-2026 Etsy themes at [Accio](https://www.accio.com/business/best-selling-wall-art-on-etsy)
- UK trade view: bold scale statement canvases, textured and mixed-media pieces, and digital art / limited editions, with emphasis on sustainability and provenance — [Trowbridge](https://www.trowbridgegallery.com/trade-art-insights/current-uk-wall-art-trends-for-2026-influencing-designers-4733)
- Photographic art is described as having grown into a full decorating trend over recent years, with expanding demand for fine art photography — [Desenio, photography art and posters](https://desenio.com/posters-prints/art-prints/photographs/)
- Watercolour is the dominant treatment in the UK wildlife and nursery segments specifically: watercolour wildlife sets, watercolour hare, watercolour bear/fox/deer nursery sets, watercolour butterfly prints — [Florence and Lavender](https://www.florenceandlavender.co.uk/collections/wildlife-art-prints); [Etsy UK](https://www.etsy.com/uk/market/british_wildlife_art); [Etsy, nursery animal print](https://www.etsy.com/market/nursery_animal_print)
- Minimal line art is live in the animal segment (highland cow line art) and the figure segment (soft hand and couple illustrations in beige/grey) — [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1839252070/highland-cow-line-art-print-highland-cow); [Desenio UK bestsellers](https://desenio.co.uk/posters-prints/top-list/)
- "Vintage retro minimalist" is used as the style descriptor on a bestselling abstract — [Design Instantly](https://designinstantly.com/best-selling-on-etsy/wall-art)
- Interiors press for 2026: bold expressive styles such as large-scale abstracts, nature-inspired prints and vintage-inspired pieces, with emphasis on texture and depth; a single large-format print in a warm muted palette can anchor a room — [About Wall Art, UK 2026 trends](https://aboutwallart.com/blogs/news-articles-home-decor-inspiration/home-decor-trends-for-2026-key-looks-for-every-room)

### Inferences
- Generation-ready style list, ranked by strength of 2026 evidence: **1. textured/impasto and visible-brushstroke abstraction; 2. vintage lithograph / antique plate restoration (aged paper, foxing, plate marks, period typefaces used as part of the image not as a quote); 3. watercolour (wet-edge, granulating, soft bleed); 4. minimal line art (single-weight, continuous line); 5. fine-art photography look (shallow depth of field, grain, muted grade); 6. block print / linocut (visible carve marks, misregistration); 7. mid-century modern (flat shapes, limited palette, slight print texture); 8. risograph (2-3 spot colours, halftone, deliberate misalignment); 9. Bauhaus/geometric; 10. Japandi (negative space, ink wash, muted wood tones); 11. art deco (fan motifs, metallics - overlaps "galactic metallic"); 12. boho (now the weakest of the list, reading as 2021 rather than 2026).**
- "Texture, story and soul" and "sustainability and provenance" are the two phrases that should most worry an AI pipeline: they are precisely the attributes AI output is weakest on and the attributes 2026 buyers are being told to look for. Styles with built-in physical artefacts (impasto, linocut, riso misregistration, aged paper) are the ones where generated output reads as authentic; flat digital vector minimalism is where it reads as cheap.
- Boho and "earthy tone" remain high-volume (35,000+/month claimed) while reading as dated in trend forecasts. That combination - high search, declining fashion - is typical of a saturated niche, which matters given the near-duplication risk recorded in CLAUDE.md.

### Gaps
- No source quantifies style demand (e.g. watercolour vs line art share of sales). The ranking above is reasoned from frequency of mention plus observed listing titles.
- Risograph specifically: no 2025-2026 marketplace source found naming riso as a selling style. Included on style-adjacency reasoning only.

## Q4. Which colour palettes recur in bestsellers?

### Takeaway
Three palettes are corroborated across independent UK and marketplace sources for 2026: warm earthy neutrals (mushroom, cashmere, creamy beige, soft khaki, washed linen), the green family (sage, smoky jade, eucalyptus, dark green as a "new neutral"), and a returning moody blue family (Patina Blue, inky blue, weathered maritime blue). Terracotta and mustard recur as the accent pair. Cool grey is explicitly described as being displaced.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 UK colour forecast: cool greys replaced by earthy browns, softened blues, botanical greens and chalky warm neutrals; "mushroom, cashmere, sage, and terracotta tones are the new foundation palette" — [RAB Art, 2026 colour forecast](https://rabart.co.uk/blogs/news/your-2026-colour-forecast-9)
- Blue returns after years of green dominance; richer, moodier blues are reshaping interiors; dark green is called a "new neutral" — [Leekes, decorating trends 2026](https://www.leekes.co.uk/blog/inspiration/decoration-trends/decorating-trends-2026-spring-summer-2026-interior-design-trends-uk-to-refresh-your-home)
- The layering palette given for UK 2026 interiors: deep greens, warm terracottas, mustard yellows, inky blues, earthy reds — [Leekes](https://www.leekes.co.uk/blog/inspiration/decoration-trends/decorating-trends-2026-spring-summer-2026-interior-design-trends-uk-to-refresh-your-home)
- UK trade, art-specific: "warm neutrals with pops of terracotta, sage, deep green and mustard work well with 2026 furniture trends" — [Trowbridge](https://www.trowbridgegallery.com/trade-art-insights/current-uk-wall-art-trends-for-2026-influencing-designers-4733)
- **Patina Blue is Etsy's 2026 Colour of the Year; Washed Linen is its first Texture of the Year** — [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/etsy-trends-2026/)
- Further 2026 colour names in the same forecast: Cloud Dancer (Pantone 2026), Transformative Teal, creamy beiges, soft khakis, smoky jade to eucalyptus, burnt mahogany, plum berry, red-brown — [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/etsy-trends-2026/)
- Printify predicts green hues as the dominant 2026 colour and names linen, raffia and terracotta as the material/colour reference set; finishes split between holographic/reflective/metallic and matte — [Printify](https://printify.com/blog/leading-trends-for-etsy-sellers-in-2026/)
- Observed bestseller palettes: abstract green and beige; beige and grey; beige and white; "vintage retro minimalist green" — [Desenio UK bestsellers](https://desenio.co.uk/posters-prints/top-list/); [Design Instantly](https://designinstantly.com/best-selling-on-etsy/wall-art)

### Inferences
- Four named palettes to encode as reusable prompt fragments:
  - **Warm neutral / washed linen:** off-white, oat, mushroom, cashmere, soft khaki, warm grey-beige. The default base.
  - **Green:** sage, smoky jade, eucalyptus, olive, deep forest ("new neutral"), on a cream ground.
  - **Patina / inky blue:** patina blue, weathered maritime blue, slate, deep navy, with off-white. Carries Etsy's colour-of-the-year endorsement, so likely under-supplied relative to coming demand.
  - **Terracotta and mustard accent:** burnt orange, terracotta, ochre, mustard, burnt mahogany, earthy red, plum berry - used as an accent *within* a neutral or green composition rather than as a whole palette.
- Black and white remains viable but is not endorsed by any 2026 forecast found; it appears in the market mainly as line art and photography. Jewel tones appear only as "deep jewel tones stepping into the spotlight" in the UK interiors trend, i.e. as a secondary not a lead.
- Patina blue plus washed-linen texture is the single most specific, most recent, most citable palette instruction available, and it comes from the marketplace itself. It is the obvious thing to over-index on for a 2026 launch.

### Gaps
- "Patina Blue" and "Washed Linen" are reported via eRank citing Etsy; I did not reach Etsy's own trend report to confirm the hex/reference or the exact wording. Worth verifying before it becomes a pipeline constant.

## Q5. Which subjects suit sets of 2-3, and is the set format actually higher value?

### Takeaway
Sets are a real and widely used higher-AOV mechanism - multi-piece sets are credited with $50-80 average orders versus single prints, and marketplaces run dedicated "pair" and "gallery wall set" merchandising. The subjects that set well are ones with a natural enumerable series: species plates, moon phases, triptych landscapes, matched abstract variations, and nursery animal trios.

### Cited Findings
- Multi-piece sets and collections are credited with driving average orders of $50-80, versus single prints, because wall-art buyers buy multiple pieces to complete a gallery wall or room theme — [MerchTitans, POD wall art guide](https://merchtitans.com/blog/print-on-demand-wall-art-guide)
- Bundle pricing (e.g. a set of 3 at $49.99) is described as dramatically increasing average order value; some sellers price a set of 3 at 20% under the cost of the three singles — [MerchTitans](https://merchtitans.com/blog/print-on-demand-wall-art-guide)
- Desenio runs a dedicated "Perfect Pair" merchandising category for posters sold in twos — [Desenio, trendy posters in pairs](https://desenio.com/g/gallery-walls/perfect-pair/)
- Desenio sells curated "poster packs" as a product type, with abstract landscape, sunset stroke and coffee packs among them — [Desenio, poster packs](https://desenio.com/posters-prints/poster-pack/)
- Live set-of-3 bundles observed: botanical/flower-market/exhibition-poster set — [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1803652889/set-of-3-prints-bundle-prints-flower); Picasso/Matisse/botanical "soft gallery wall" set — [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1594308345/soft-gallery-wall-art-set-of-3-prints); vintage butterfly-and-botanical print set — [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/294039677/vintage-butterfly-and-botanical-print)
- Sets scale beyond 3 in practice: "gallery wall prints set of 6" is a live search variant — [Etsy, gallery wall prints set](https://www.etsy.com/market/gallery_wall_prints_set)
- In nursery, set-of-3 and set-of-5 are the default unit, not the exception: 5-pack woodland/safari framed sets, 3-piece lion/elephant/giraffe sets, 3-piece bear/fox/deer sets — [Amazon, ArtbyHannah woodland 5-pack](https://www.amazon.com/clp/B0BBPXKRNH); [Amazon, 3-piece safari set](https://www.amazon.com/clp/B0CNPCWP9J)
- A UK retailer merchandises gallery wall sets as a standing collection — [Pretty Average, gallery wall sets](https://www.pretty-average.co.uk/collections/gallery-wall-sets)

### Inferences
- Set-friendly subject structures, i.e. what to generate as series rather than singles: **species trios** (three British birds, three mushrooms, three butterflies, three ferns); **moon phases** (3 or 5); **triptych landscape** (one scene split, or three variations of the same horizon); **matched abstract variations** (same palette, same mark language, three compositions); **nursery animal trios** (the market standard); **botanical trios in matching vases**; **three city plans** for someone who has lived in three places; **three-stage sequences** (sunrise/noon/dusk; spring/summer/autumn of the same tree); **pair formats specifically** (sun and moon, two facing figures, two matched abstracts, left/right of a split scene).
- Generating in series also directly addresses the near-duplication risk in CLAUDE.md, but only if handled carefully: a set is *intentional* close variation sold as one SKU, whereas 89% near-duplication is unintentional close variation sold as many SKUs. The pipeline should produce N-image sets as a single listing, and must not let set variants leak out as separate listings - that is the failure mode that produced 424k non-selling listings on the wall-art branch.
- The $50-80 AOV figure is vendor-sourced and US-dollar; treat the *direction* as reliable and the *number* as indicative. The 20%-discount-versus-singles convention is a usable pricing rule.

### Gaps
- No marketplace-published data on set-versus-single conversion rate or revenue share. All AOV claims trace to print-on-demand vendor blogs.

## Q6. Nursery and children's art: specific subjects and styles

### Takeaway
Nursery is the highest-volume claimed category and the most formulaic, which makes it the best fit for a generation pipeline. The format is fixed (sets of 3 or 5), the style is fixed (soft watercolour, boho-neutral, occasionally line art), and the subject sets are near-standardised: safari trio (lion, elephant, giraffe), woodland trio (bear, fox, deer), plus butterflies, jungle birds and alphabet/number charts.

### Cited Findings

## Q8. Does AI-generated art sell acceptably, and where does it look cheap or get rejected?

### Takeaway
AI-generated image art sells on Etsy and is explicitly permitted there with a disclosure label, but it is outright banned on at least one print marketplace (INPRNT) and conditionally restricted on Society6, and the bottom of the Etsy digital-art market is already commoditised to $2-13 for bundles of thousands of images. The failure modes that get noticed are anatomical errors, visible text, and low-resolution output that shows pixels in print - all of which are avoidable by subject choice.

### Cited Findings
**It is permitted and it sells**
- Etsy did not ban AI-generated art. It revised seller policy with "Creativity Standards" requiring sellers to label products by degree of human involvement; AI-assisted designs must be labelled "Designed by" — [TechCrunch, Etsy new seller policy 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/etsy-new-seller-policy-2024-generative-ai); [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/etsy-shopping-rules-ai-warning-online-shoppers-1923399); [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/ai-image-video/etsy-will-let-you-sell-ai-art-on-its-platform-but-theres-a-catch)
- Observed bestsellers in the Etsy printable-art category are explicitly AI-era mass bundles, which sell: a "Mega Printable Wall Art Bundle | PLR + MRR White Label License | 300 DPI" at $2 marked Best Seller; a "60,000 Digital Wall Art Bundle" at $5 marked Best Seller; a "25,000 Mega Bundle" at $13 marked Best Seller; a "1000+ Printable Wall Art Bundle | 300 DPI" at $2 marked Best Seller — [Design Instantly](https://designinstantly.com/best-selling-on-etsy/wall-art)

**Where it is rejected**
- INPRNT's code of conduct bars uploading, using, sharing, buying, selling or distributing any artwork generated via an automated process using AI or machine learning — a flat ban — [summarised via marketplace policy comparison](https://www.yeschat.ai/blog-Is-It-LEGAL-to-SELL-AI-Generated-Art-on-Etsy-RedBubble-Society6-Other-Marketplaces-31349)
- Society6's community guidelines state artwork created primarily by AI without meaningful human input does not meet standards and expect demonstrated creative contribution; secondary sources disagree about how strictly this is written and enforced — [Toolify, Society6 AI art policy](https://www.toolify.ai/ai-news/discover-the-truth-society6s-ai-art-policy-revealed-1269148) vs. [Toolify, Society6 welcomes AI art](https://www.toolify.ai/ai-news/unlocking-the-potential-society6-welcomes-ai-art-1338145). **These two sources directly contradict each other and both are low-quality AI-news aggregators. Society6's position should be read from its own guidelines before relying on it.**
- Society6 has shut down artist accounts, per an artist-side account of the episode — [CatCoq](https://catcoq.com/blog/society6-shutting-down-artist-accounts)
- Etsy faces rising pushback from long-term sellers over its AI content policies, including how clearly AI products are labelled and governed, and competing platforms that ban AI goods outright are attracting some Etsy sellers — [Yahoo Finance, Is Etsy losing sellers over AI content rules](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/etsy-etsy-losing-sellers-over-151039214.html)
- A claim that over 17,000 Etsy listings were removed in early 2025 for AI-disclosure policy violations, attributed to "internal data obtained by the Etsy Sellers' Alliance", with automated detection producing frequent false positives — [ShieldMyShop](https://www.shieldmyshop.com/blog/2026-04-09-selling-ai-art-on-etsy-copyright-rules-disclosure-2026). **I could not corroborate this figure anywhere else and the source is an SEO blog selling shop-protection services. Do not rely on it.**

**Where it looks cheap**
- On a stock platform, AI images with visible defects - the example given is seven fingers - draw buyer complaints and refund demands after purchase, when the buyer inspects quality detail; contributors with excessive refunds can have accounts temporarily or permanently blocked — [Adobe Community, quality issues on my AI image](https://community.adobe.com/t5/stock-contributors-discussions/quality-issues-on-my-ai-image/m-p/14255630)
- Print-quality complaints in the poster market centre on visible pixels and a product that "looks cheap" — [Trustpilot UK review of a print seller](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/funkycurator.com)
- The 2026 Etsy buyer mood is explicitly for products that "feel human, with texture, story and soul" — [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/etsy-trends-2026/) — and UK trade demand stresses provenance — [Trowbridge](https://www.trowbridgegallery.com/trade-art-insights/current-uk-wall-art-trends-for-2026-influencing-designers-4733)

### Inferences
- The commercial reading: AI-generated wall art unquestionably sells, but the segment it most obviously sells in - mega-bundles at $2-13 - is a race to the bottom with no margin. The opportunity is not "can AI art sell" but "can AI art be presented as a curated, UK-specific, well-palette-managed range at a normal print price", which is a merchandising and differentiation problem, not a generation problem.
- Subject choice is the main quality control available. **Low AI risk:** botanical, abstract, landscape, coastal, celestial, textures, animals rendered in watercolour or line art, nursery animals. **High AI risk:** human hands and faces (the Desenio-style hand and couple illustrations are exactly the bestselling subject AI is worst at), any image containing legible text or labels (which rules out naive generation of mushroom identification charts, anatomical plates and map labels - those need the text composited separately, not generated), architectural perspective and repeated structural detail, mechanical subjects (cars, aircraft, instruments) where buyers know the real shape, and anything with countable anatomy.
- Disclosure is cheap and should be treated as mandatory on Etsy: "Designed by" labelling. The INPRNT ban means that platform is simply unavailable to this pipeline, and Society6 should be assumed restrictive until its own guidelines are read.
- Resolution discipline matters more than style: the one concrete buyer complaint pattern found is visible pixels at print size. 300 DPI at the largest size sold is the floor, and the "oversized/statement" demand signal means that largest size is big.
- Styles with physical artefacts baked in (impasto, linocut, riso misregistration, aged lithograph paper, watercolour granulation) are the ones where generated output reads as "texture, story and soul" rather than as flat digital output. This aligns the quality-risk mitigation exactly with the Q3 style ranking, which is convenient: the styles 2026 buyers want are the styles that hide AI tells.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on AI-art conversion or return rates versus human-made art on any print marketplace. The refund evidence is from Adobe Stock (stock licensing, not prints) and is anecdotal forum testimony.
- Etsy's own Creativity Standards page was not fetched directly; the policy description rests on press coverage (TechCrunch, Newsweek, Tom's Guide), which is consistent across those three.
- Displate's current AI position is unclear. One summary says it accepts AI art with disclosure recommended, but I found no primary Displate policy text. Treat as unknown.
- No evidence found on whether UK buyers specifically react differently to AI art than US buyers.
