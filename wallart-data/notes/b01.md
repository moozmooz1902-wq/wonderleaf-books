# Batch 01 — bestselling-wall-art

## 0080 "A Window View Of Paris In The Style Of Art Nouveau 4" (3:4 portrait)
COMPOSITION — **frame-within-frame**. Dark casement windows open outward and
occupy the full left and right edges, making a proscenium. Viewer is INSIDE
looking out: intimacy and aspiration at once. Four depth planes stacked:
(1) interior rug + round table bottom-centre, (2) wrought-iron balcony rail,
(3) Paris rooftops, (4) sky + tree canopy. Still life AND landscape in one
picture, which doubles the subject matter per design.
PALETTE — 5 families, all desaturated, warm-dominant: terracotta/rust (cloth,
rug, geraniums, chimney pots) · olive-sage (foliage) · cream-bone (facades,
cloud) · dark umber (window frames) · muted slate-blue (roofs).
MEDIUM — flat gouache-like fills, **every form carries a dark contour line**
(Art Nouveau / woodcut logic). Foliage is clustered flat leaf-shapes, never
individual leaves. No gradients, no photographic texture.
WHY IT WORKS — it sells a *window the room doesn't have*. Travel aspiration
without a photograph. Decorative density rewards a second look.
REUSABLE AS — `{interior casement window} + {place beyond} + {still life on
table} + {movement}`. Swap Paris for any UK town. No text, no hard geometry.

## 0082 "Snowdonia National Park Wales Colourful Mountain Illustration" (3:4)
**The single most important image for our UK place plan.**
COMPOSITION — pure landscape. No text, no figures, no buildings. Valley recedes
to a distant peak by overlapping planes. Horizon sits at ~8% from the top; the
sky is a thin pale band. The land IS the picture. Busiest, most saturated
material in the bottom third.
TECHNIQUE — the soul of it: the whole surface is **flat colour facets filled
with dense parallel hatching that follows the slope contours**. Line direction
does the modelling; there is no tonal shading and no blending anywhere. It is
map-contour logic applied to a painting, so it reads simultaneously as
hand-drawn and as geological survey.
PALETTE — deliberately NON-naturalistic in the foreground: coral, hot pink,
mustard, lilac, mint, cobalt, salmon. But **the distant peaks are naturalistic**
blue-grey and green. Realism increases with distance. That gradient is what
keeps it feeling like a real place instead of an abstract.
WHY IT WORKS — a named real place rendered joyfully: memory plus decor. The
wide colour range means it matches almost any room. Reads as colour blocks from
across a room, rewards close viewing with the hatching.
REUSABLE AS — `{UK fell/valley} contour-hatched illustration`. 214 Wainwrights
+ 282 Munros + 186 Nuttalls + 15 National Parks. This is Phase 1 proven.

## 0084 "Bird In Nature Barn Owl 3" (**SQUARE**, not 3:4)
COMPOSITION — **hero subject + decorative habitat**, a different grammar from
0082. Single bird centred-left in the upper third, perched on a stone pedestal.
A meadow of dried seedheads and dandelion clocks frames it and runs off all
four edges. Flat banded hills behind, flat slate dusk sky.
TECHNIQUE — collage / cut-paper layering; hills are torn-edge flat bands.
**Deliberate detail gradient: the subject is the only sharp thing.** The owl
carries fine herringbone feather barring and hand-dotted speckles while the
whole environment stays flat. That gradient is what makes the subject read
instantly from a distance.
PALETTE — slate navy sky · cream/bone/oatmeal/sand dominant · **warm rust-orange
accent at roughly 5% of area** (wing bar, scattered flowers) · muted petrol
blue. The warm-neutral + single hot accent formula.
WHY IT WORKS — British wildlife has a strong UK emotional pull; dusk mood reads
calm rather than cute; the small orange accent gives pop without loudness.
REUSABLE AS — `{British species} perched in {habitat}, cut-paper collage, flat
banded ground, warm neutral + rust accent`. 636 BOU species, 107 mammals.

## Cross-cutting so far
- Two distinct composition grammars already: **framed view** (0080) and
  **hero subject + flat habitat** (0084), plus **pure terrain** (0082).
- Warm-neutral palette with one saturated accent recurs.
- **Nothing is photographic.** Every one is a made mark.
- **No gradients anywhere.** Flat fill, contour line, or hatching.
- Format is NOT uniform: 0080/0082 are 3:4, 0084 is square.
