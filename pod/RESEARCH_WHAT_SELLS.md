# What is actually selling — eBay UK, 30 Aug 2026

Titles and sold counts below are copied verbatim from eBay UK search
results. Sold counts are the ones eBay shows on active listings; the full
sold history needs a sign-in, which I did not use.

## The correction I needed

I had assumed several versions of one subject compete with each other. The
seller's own trading says otherwise, and the search pages agree: a buyer
scrolls until a picture appeals, so the eighth lion catches the buyer who
did not like the first seven. Coverage of a search beats restraint.

What still holds: **the picture has to actually differ**. Not the palette
of the same picture — a different idea of what a lion print is.

look genuinely different.**

Sub-variants visible: suit, tweed suit, red suit, Victorian, gangster,
smoking a cigar, with whisky, monocle, top hat, bow tie, military uniform.

## Price: £4.99 A4 unframed is the volume seller

    Hedgehogs 5x7 Print on Matte Paper Cute Woodland Animal      £4.99    35 sold

## Cars sell, but on brand names

    Nissan Skyline GT-R Wall Art Print A4 JDM Car Poster Garage Decor
    Ferrari LaFerrari Poster Super Sport Car Large Wall Art Print
    McLaren P1 Super Car Large Poster Wall Art Print Size A4 A2 A1 A0
    Subaru Impreza WRX STI Poster JDM Rally Car Wall Art A4 A3
    BMW E46 M3 Poster | Splash Art Car Wall Print A4 A3
    1969 Dodge Charger Poster Classic American Muscle Car Wall Art
    CoCo fashion Car Designer Wall Art Poster Print Picture  £3.49-£13.99  167+ sold

Nearly every car listing names a manufacturer and model. Those are
trademarks. Plenty of sellers do it; that does not make it safe, and a
manufacturer complaint can take an account rather than a listing.

**The lower-risk middle ground**, which the same page shows working:
generic body type plus a style — `Classic Hot Hatch`, `American Muscle`,
`JDM Rally`, `Le Mans Racer`, `1960s Roadster` — in **blueprint, cutaway,
splash art or watercolour**. The style is what people are buying in those
listings; the badge is the search term. Worth testing both and letting the
numbers decide, with the brand-named ones on one store only.

    Tiswas Mens Ladies T-Shirt Funny Retro Classic TV Programme   27+ sold

All three are film or TV property. Off limits. But the non-IP formats on the

    Undiagnosed But Something Ain't Right Tee Funny Mental Health   41+ sold
    Everything Hurts and I'm Tired
    I Don't Know How To Act My Age
    I Fix Stuff and I Know Things
    Waiting on Parts

These are **broad relatable humour** — no occupation, no hobby, no breed.
Our 50,740 are all niche-targeted: `My Nan Is A Bouncer`, `Carp Fishing
Legend`. Both work, but we have none of the broad kind, and the broad kind
is what shows sold counts on page one.

Also present and worth copying as a mechanic, not as content: wordplay and
double meaning (`I Have Sex Daily` / dyslexia), and the skeleton-waiting
visual gag.

## What this changes

1. **Concepts are the multiplier, not palettes.** `animal × in a suit`,
   `animal × bathroom pun`, `place × railway poster` each turn one bank into
   a full catalogue of pictures that genuinely differ.
2. **Places are the biggest unoccupied space** and we already hold the data.
3. **£4.99 A4 unframed** is where volume happens.
4. **Broad humour is missing from the tee catalogue** and is the format with
   visible sold counts.
5. **Car brands are the one place to be deliberate** rather than automatic.
